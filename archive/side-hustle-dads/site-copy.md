## My story: why I do this

I'm a husband and a dad to two young girls, and I work a full-time job. Through several different jobs over more than a decade, I've kept starting side hustles. Most of them failed, and I'll tell you what they taught me. Everything I share is for the man who just wants his family taken care of.

## The question I get

**Heading:** If my side hustles make this much, why do I still have a *day job?*
**Lead:** Because I don't know *what tomorrow brings.*

If I were single with no kids, I'd probably do this full time. But I'm a husband, a father and the sole provider for my family, and I take that seriously. I won't put all my eggs in a side hustle basket.

1. **It's my safety net.** A steady paycheck, plus health and medical benefits for my family.
2. **I know what it's like without one.** I've lived paycheck to paycheck and thought about money constantly. Now I can save, and I'm playing catch up for my family's future.
3. **I'm good at it, and I like it.** I've spent years perfecting my craft and my career, and I enjoy the work.
4. **Why stop what's working?** I can handle both and give my job the time it needs. If I left, I'd probably just start more side hustles, and the job fills that time nicely.

Close: Keep your job. It takes the pressure off, so you can try a few things and see what works.

Example phone screens: "Have you asked a stranger for money?" and an example result "Fix one thing first." with five bars and a next step: "Ask 5 strangers for a booked time or a pre-order."

**Five tests. The weakest one decides.** A side hustle breaks at its weakest point, so one zero caps your verdict.
1. Already has buyers: Do strangers already pay for something like it?
2. Already has a door: Is there a place buyers already look?
3. Fits your week: Do you have the hours?
4. Safe to be wrong: Could you stop in a weekend?
5. The math holds: Do your numbers add up?

**And five hard stops come first:** Your work agreement · Reachable during work hours · Costly license or gear · More than you can lose · Home doesn't know yet

## Questions you might have

- **I barely have time. Is this realistic?** The scorecard checks that first. If an idea doesn't fit around your job and your family, it will say so.
- **What if my employer doesn't allow it?** It's the first thing the scorecard asks about. Check your work agreement before you start anything. This isn't legal advice.
- **What if my side hustle fails?** Most of mine did. The scorecard helps you find out cheaply, before you've put in a lot.
- **Will this make me money?** I can't promise that, and nobody honest can. It helps you choose better ideas and skip some of the misses.
- **What happens to my email?** I'll send you the link, and I might send a short note now and then. Unsubscribe any time. Read the privacy page.

## Voice rules that were enforced by tests (see `tests/brand.test.js`)

- No em dashes or en dashes.
- No guru language: "unlock", "game changer", "level up", "lambo", and similar.
- Say "side hustle", never "side business" or "side gig".
