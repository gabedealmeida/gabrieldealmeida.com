My story

Why I do this.

I'm a husband and a dad to two young girls, and I work a full-time job. Through several different jobs over more than a decade, I've kept starting side hustles. Most of them failed, and I'll tell you what they taught me. Everything I share is for the man who just wants his family taken care of.

  1. High school

    Side hustles, then a company

    I tried several side hustles first. Then I started a monthly subscription company that was profitable from day one.

  2. College

    Led a team, then sold it

    I ran it with four employees and 15 community moderators. I sold it to another company while I was still in college.

  3. After the sale

    Co-founded a second brand

    I co-founded a new brand with the company that bought mine, and I was its growth director. The community passed 50,000 members.

  4. Today

    Side hustles, and a job

    Most of my side hustles failed. The ones that worked already had buyers, a door already open, and fit around my job. I still have a full-time job, on purpose.

The question I get

If my side hustles make this much, why do I still have a day job?

Because I don't know what tomorrow brings.

If I were single with no kids, I'd probably do this full time. But I'm a husband, a father and the sole provider for my family, and I take that seriously. I won't put all my eggs in a side hustle basket.

  1. 01

    It's my safety net.

    A steady paycheck, plus health and medical benefits for my family.

  2. 02

    I know what it's like without one.

    I've lived paycheck to paycheck and thought about money constantly. Now I can save, and I'm playing catch up for my family's future.

  3. 03

    I'm good at it, and I like it.

    I've spent years perfecting my craft and my career, and I enjoy the work.

  4. 04

    Why stop what's working?

    I can handle both and give my job the time it needs. If I left, I'd probably just start more side hustles, and the job fills that time nicely.
