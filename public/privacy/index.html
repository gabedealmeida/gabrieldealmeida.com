Privacy

Last updated October 10, 2026

What I collect

Your first name and email address, when you sign up to use Score My Side Hustle Idea. That's it. The answers you give in the scorecard stay on your device. They aren't sent to me or to anyone else.

Why

To let you in to the scorecard, and to send you a short email now and then about starting a side hustle while keeping your job.

Who handles it

  • Kit, the email service that stores your name and email and sends my emails.
  • Railway, which hosts this site and keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address and browser type.

I don't sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set cookies and doesn't run analytics or ad trackers. To remember where you left off, the scorecard saves your answers and your first name in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can clear them any time by clearing your browser's site data, or by choosing "Start over" or "Score another idea" in the test.

Unsubscribing and deleting

Every email I send has an unsubscribe link. If you'd like your information deleted, reply to any of my emails and ask.

Children

This site is for adults and isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, I'll update the date at the top.
