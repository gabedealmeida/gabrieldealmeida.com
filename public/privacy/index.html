Privacy

Last updated October 10, 2026

What I collect

Your email address when you sign up, and your first name if you choose to give it inside the scorecard. That's it. The answers you give in the scorecard stay on your device. They aren't sent to me or to anyone else.

Why

To let you in to the scorecard, and to send you a weekly note with tips, stories and side hustles worth a look. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Who handles it

  • Flodesk, the email service that stores your name and email and sends my emails.
  • Plausible, run on my own server, which counts visits and button taps without cookies or personal details.
  • Railway, which hosts this site and keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address and browser type.

I don't sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set cookies and doesn't run ad trackers. It uses Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count visits and which buttons get tapped, such as how many people finish the scorecard. Plausible doesn't use cookies or follow you around the web, and it never receives your name, your email or your answers. To remember where you left off, the scorecard saves your answers, your first name and the email you signed up with in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can clear them any time by clearing your browser's site data, or by choosing "Start over" or "Score another idea" in the test.

Unsubscribing and deleting

Every email I send has an unsubscribe link. If you'd like your information deleted, reply to any of my emails and ask.

Children

This site is for adults and isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, I'll update the date at the top.
