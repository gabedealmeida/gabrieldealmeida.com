Privacy

Last updated November 19, 2026

What I collect

Nothing about you directly. This site has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no way to enter your email. I don't collect email addresses here.

Following and messaging me

The buttons on this site open Instagram, where you can follow me or send me a message. What you share there is handled by Instagram under its own rules, and I only see what you choose to send me.

Who handles what

  • Plausible, run on my own server, which counts visits and button taps without cookies or personal details.
  • Railway, which hosts this site and keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address and browser type.
  • Instagram, only if you follow or message me there.

I don't sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set cookies and doesn't run ad trackers. It uses Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count visits and which buttons get tapped, such as how many people tap the message button. Plausible doesn't use cookies or follow you around the web, and it never receives your name or any other personal details.

Deleting

If you message me on Instagram and want the conversation deleted, ask me there.

Children

This site is for adults and isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, I'll update the date at the top.
